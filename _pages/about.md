Hello! Welcome to my site. I am a PhD Candidate in Statistics at the Colorado School fo Mines. My research interests are, broadly speaking, computational statistics and mathematics with applications in hydrology and environmental modeling. I have worked on projects involving new methodologies in spatial statistics, deriving error bounds using kernal approximation methods, and differentiable process-based models (dPBM) in hydrology. I am actively looking for more opportunities to expand my horizons, please don't hesitate to reach out!
